# Guns Pack Attribution

"Guns Asset Pack" by styloo - https://styloo.itch.io/ - CC0 (public domain,
no attribution required, credited here anyway).

Only 4 of the pack's 22 models are used:
- **mac10.glb** - Mac-10 viewmodel/pickup/gun store display
- **shotgun.glb** - Shotgun viewmodel/pickup/gun store display
- **pew.glb** - Pistol viewmodel/gun store display
- **ammobox_low.glb** - Ammo pickup model

The rest (AK47, AWP, rocket launcher, grenades, flashbangs, smoke) aren't
wired to any existing weapon/mechanic and were left out.
